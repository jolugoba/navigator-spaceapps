## End card (27–30 s)
- Logo: `guacalita_final.html` (or the game's icon).
- Text: **Navigator · NASA Space Apps 2026**
- Small line: *Orbits: NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics · Mission facts: NASA Science, NASA/JPL.*

## Recording checklist
- [ ] Phone recording in landscape, screen capture at the highest quality the phone allows.
- [ ] Second phone filming the AR shot (0–4 s and 15–22 s).
- [ ] Turn on "Do not disturb" and close other apps before recording.
- [ ] Record each shot 2–3 times and keep the best.
- [ ] Captions burned in, same timing as the SRT files.
- [ ] Export 1080p, under the contest's file size limit.

## Before submitting (from `SPACE_APPS_SUBMISSION.md` §7)
- [ ] Full run on a phone: design → window → flight → science → debrief, in EN and ES.
- [ ] Two-phone multiplayer test with QR.
- [ ] Revoke and replace any Meshy key that appeared in chats.
